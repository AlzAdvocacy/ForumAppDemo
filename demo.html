Open the appPersona links

Pick a person and a moment in the week

Each link opens the app as one fictional attendee with the demo clock set to a particular day and time, so you can show what the app looks like before Hill Day, during it, and on the way home. Point a phone camera at the code, or copy the link into a text message.

The clock is simulated: Hill Day is always "today", Day 1 is yesterday and Day 3 is tomorrow. Opening a link resets the clock on that phone. Tap the AIM logo three times inside the app to change it again. Every name, meeting and schedule is fictional.

Set up a demo phone

  1. iPhone: open a link in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, then Add. Open the app from the Home Screen.
  2. Android: open the link in Chrome and tap Install when it appears, or use the menu and choose Add to Home screen.
  3. Open the app once while you have a signal. After that it opens without one.

Run the demo

  • Demo controls: tap the AIM logo at the top of the app three times. Tap Done to hide them.
  • Change the moment: the clock picker in the controls has the same presets as this page, plus "Hill Day at the real time".
  • Schedule change in 10 seconds gives you time to set the phone down. The banner appears, the phone buzzes on Android, and the change shows on Home and Meetings.
  • Airplane mode keeps the whole schedule on screen and shows the offline banner.
  • Reset demo data in the controls puts everything back to the start.

This page and the app are public at this address. Use only fictional data here. Real advocate names, phone numbers or schedules should never go in this repository.
